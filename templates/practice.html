Ideas to try

  • SELECT name, salary FROM employees ORDER BY salary DESC LIMIT 5; — the five best-paid people.
  • SELECT dept_id, COUNT(*) FROM employees GROUP BY dept_id; — headcount per department (by id).
  • SELECT strftime('%Y-%m', sale_date) AS month, SUM(amount) FROM sales GROUP BY month; — sales by month.
